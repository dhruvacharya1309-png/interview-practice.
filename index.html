Before we start

This practice uses your microphone. Your speech is turned into text by your browser's built-in speech feature. In Chrome, that speech is processed by Google. We measure your pace, pauses, filler words and pitch to write your report.

We don't keep the recording or the transcript after you close this page. Your report may mention things that were hard to measure. It may also note answers that sounded read aloud or scripted. These are things to review, not a verdict on you.
